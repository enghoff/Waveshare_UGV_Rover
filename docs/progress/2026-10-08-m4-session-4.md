## The owner in the way of a 1.8 m goal

From (-18.32, -13.79) to (-18.57, -15.59), heading -72 degrees, a geometry
goal's drive. 1.8 m is beyond `NEAR_GOAL_M` (1.5 m), so it was one Nav2 goal,
and the live-scan watch of
[session 13](2026-10-07-m3-session-13.md) does not look at those. The planner,
whose map has no person on it, sent the same path every second from 17:12:36 to
17:13:03; the controller, which does see them, would not drive into them and
turned on the spot; it arrived when the owner moved. The stall watch would have
ended it at 25 s without 0.5 m of progress.

**The fix** (`ros_nav/nav_moves.py`, `route_watch`, `seen_on_the_route`,
`past_it`): every second the next 1.0 m of the route Nav2 is following is
checked on the live costmap as a near goal's straight line is -- the body swept
along it, lethal cells only -- and only for what the planner's own costmap does
not have, so a door frame the route passes close to is not taken for a person.
Blocked, the goal stops and the rover goes to a point 1.4 m along the route as a
near goal, which waits 3 s for the way and then goes round in straight legs,
and then on to the goal, at most three times. No way round hands the goal back
as blocked.

**Reproduced before the fix** in `ros_nav/test_way_round.py`, the session-13
room with this goal: a planner that cannot see the owner and a controller that
turns on the spot while they are on its next 0.6 m. With the code as it was, the
rover turned until the stall watch ended the goal and never waited or went
round, as on the rover. With the fix: stepped in front of, it stops within two
seconds, waits, goes round them 0.25 m or more away and arrives within the near
goals' own 0.22 m; one who steps away is waited for and driven on to; someone on
the goal itself is given up as blocked, not turned at; with nobody there it is
one Nav2 goal as before; something on the planner's map as well is not stopped
for. ros_nav 623 tests pass. Deployed at 77b171d; navigation came back settled
with the position trusted.
