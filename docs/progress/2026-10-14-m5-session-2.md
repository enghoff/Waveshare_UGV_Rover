# M5 session 2: the lidar watch finds two of seven staged changes, and step 3 of the what/where plan passes its test with five

**The second counted M5 session ran cleanly, but its staging was harder for
the lidar. By M5's count the watch found two of seven staged events: the
armchair's and the cabinet's new places. The armchair's old place and the
stool came out small; the cabinet's old place, a box and a step ladder were
missed.** Step 3 of [prediction in what and where streams](../plans/what-where-streams.md),
version 2, fixed before the session (`0293ecb`, 12:22; the session began at
12:39), joins the watch's geometry to the camera's appearance. On the same
scans it found five of the seven, named all it reported right, and raised no
false alarm the watch did not. **Step 3 passes its test.** That is one session.

## What it does not show

- **One session.** Step 3's rate is not known from it.
- **Fixed after session 1.** Version 2 was fixed after seeing session 1, the
  shakedown and the October 11 recordings. Session 2 is the first evidence it
  was not fixed against.
- **Places read after the session.** The staged places were read off the
  scans and pictures, not from step 3's events.
- **The ladder.** Its place was taken from the lidar comparison, against the
  wall by the dining table, rather than from one rough photo-based estimate
  0.95 m away. Step 3's find lies 0.23 m from the place chosen. Its pictures
  are the ladder, so the find is real; with the other estimate it would not
  have counted.
- **What step 3 missed.** The box was seen twice, three seconds apart, which
  is not persistence. The stool was not new enough to the house.
- **Identity** (criterion 4) has not been counted for this session.
