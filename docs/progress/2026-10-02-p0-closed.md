([the decision](../decisions/p0-measures-the-hardware.md)). M0b was retired the day

## What P0 leaves for later

- **The check answers rarely**: 3 answers in 27 attempts. From 1.0 to 1.6 m, the area it
  reads is 25 to 30 degrees tall in a depth picture 40 degrees tall, with two calibrated
  tilts. A third calibrated tilt, or reading from further away, would answer more often.
- **The depth camera drops off USB.** The supply rail stayed flat while it did, so the
  cable, port or camera is suspected ([the soak](2026-10-02-oak-usb-drops.md)).
- **Small objects are never found.** Bottles at 1.5 to 3 m fall under the region filter's
  minimum area, which is 0.4% of the picture, or outside the twelve largest regions kept
  per look.
- **Bearing claims on moving looks are still too small**, so
  [R-WS-10](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-10) stays `failing`. Still looks are
  close to what is claimed.
- **M3's twenty supervised autonomy sessions** remain P3's work. Autonomous runs no longer
  have a battery floor ([the decision](../decisions/autonomous-runs-have-no-battery-floor.md)).
