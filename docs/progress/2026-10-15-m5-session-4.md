# M5 session 4: the step paired an armchair's move and declined it for a stale record of both armchairs, and the test cannot pass

**The staged test of a record following its moved thing does not pass for the
second time, and this time by construction: the step acted on nothing, so the
store with it and the store without it are the same.** It was asked once, about
the plain armchair, the first staged move in any session that the shadow runner
paired at both ends. It declined because `object:379`, a record made on
2026-10-03 that holds pictures of both black armchairs, looked as much like the
arrival (0.83 against 0.82) and stood nearer it. The red bean bag came from a
room the rover never enters, so it had no place before the staging to be moved
from. [R-WS-17](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-17) stays proposed.

**What sessions 3 and 4 show together is that the record store, not the move
step, is what fails.** About 480 placed records stand in and around the living
room for a few dozen objects, a record of where something stood days ago is as
live as one of where it stands now, and the step has to get past that clutter
to act. The owner's view the same day was that rearranging everyday things in a
home is the ordinary case and a world model that cannot cope with it is not
useful; the work moves to [a world model kept per visit](../plans/a-world-model-kept-per-visit.md).

## The session

`session4-1015`, 11:12-11:31, planned in `experiments/m5_changes/staged/session4-planned.json`
before the run (`273c132`). Loop A, the wait, a 3.5-minute run (211 s, 12.7 m,
18 actions, no failures), loop B, home. Nobody was in the living room. The
rover drove home by itself and arrived 15 cm from its spot, and no stop's
position check went past the session's limits.

New in this session, the camera was aimed all round from every stop inside its
dwell: four aimed looks, recorded without a resolver pass. 23 of 32 were taken
in loop A and 19 of 32 in loop B. The rest were refused because the rover's own
looking held the camera for up to 12 s against the sweep's 5 s wait.

## What was staged

The plan was to move the red bean bag about 1 m and the plain armchair about
3.5 m, each to a place chosen so that no record looking as much like it stood
nearer (`experiments/m5_changes/move_targets.py`), and to move only those whose
loop A pictures went mostly to one record placed near them (`session.py seen`).
After loop A the check passed the bean bag and failed the armchair, whose seven
pictures went to eight records, and the owner was asked to move the bean bag
only.

Two things went differently:

- **The red bean bag was not in the living room.** It lives in a room the
  rover cannot reach and is brought out only for staging. The check's three
  pictures at its place were of the empty floor there, filed to `object:450`:
  the check counted any record placed near the place, not the thing's own. The
  bean bag was put down at about (-16.65, -13.43), the change watch's arrival,
  0.4 m from the planned spot.
- **The plain armchair was moved too**, from (-19.10, -16.74) to about
  (-19.37, -14.28), beside stop 2 rather than at the planned spot 1 m
  further on; the message to leave it crossed with the staging. The
  photographs from loop B show it without the throw.

## The step's one decision

The change watch logged the armchair's departure and arrival in loop B, and
the shadow runner paired them at 11:27:24 (cosine 0.82). The step took
`object:371` as the record at the old place, placed 0.66 m from the departure,
and declined:

> object:379 looks as much like the arrival (0.83 against 0.82) and stands
> 1.55 m from it, against 2.39 m for object:371

`object:379` stands at (-18.71, -15.68), where neither armchair is now. By the
labels of sessions 1 and 2 it holds five pictures of each armchair: it is the
mixed record that failed M5's criterion 4 on 2026-10-14. The rule that stopped
the step exists to keep it from moving one armchair's record onto the other,
and here it did what it was built to do with a record that should not have been
there.

The planning tool had predicted no such rival for the plain armchair, because
it compared the records with the armchair's labelled pictures from session 3,
not with the pictures the arrival would bring.

## Not counted

The labelled count was not run. Its question is whether the step keeps more
trials than a replay without it, and with no record moved the two stores are
the same, so the answer is no whatever the labels say. Labels for this session
are still worth making for the benchmark the next plan needs.
