# M5 scene 1: the armchairs' new places found, four false reports from a bad pose in the bedroom, and a blind spot

**M5's first scripted scene, on 2026-10-11 afternoon, found every furniture-sized
change the owner made while the rover was out of the room, missed the ones made
while it was parked in the room watching, and logged four false ones in the
bedroom where navigation's pose had gone half a metre out.** The false ones are
now refused (e3963ea): the change watch drops a scan whose far hits miss the
mapper's own map. The blind spot is open: the fix tried for it brought back
false alarms on replay and was not deployed.

## What was staged

The owner, at home, made the changes and said what they were; the bedroom's were
read off photographs, and the precise positions still want the owner's word.

- **Living room** (the charger's room, which the owner calls the hall): two
  black armchairs moved and a pink bucket added, about 13:10, while the rover
  was parked in the room after control 7. Then, while the rover was in the
  bedroom (14:18-14:24), the armchairs moved again, to either side of the
  rover's parking spot.
- **Bedroom**, made before the rover went in: pillows put on the floor beside
  the bed, and a wooden stool tipped on its side by the wardrobe.

## The false reports: a pose half a metre out

Read off the recording, the scan fitted the map within 10 cm at 0.90-0.99 down
the corridor, slid to 0.74 over the two metres into the bedroom, fell to
0.28-0.40 in the turn on arrival, and at 14:18:45 the mapper moved its
correction 0.8 m and 7 degrees and it fell to 0.23; drawn over the map, the
bedroom's walls lay half a metre and a few degrees off. Navigation went on
calling the position trusted. Its own check
([R-NAV-14](../requirements/navigation.md#r-nav-14)) said it could not say, 82%
where it thought it was and no better nearby, which the watch does not pause on.
The watch's own fit check judged the scans against what it had seen, and a
bedroom seen all day was solid near enough everywhere to let them through.

Against the mapper's map, the hits beyond 1.5 m, walls mostly, separate the
cases: good poses on every recording of the two days fit at a median of 0.95 to
1.0, with at most 2% of scans under 0.6; the armchairs beside the rover at 0.87;
the bad bedroom pose at 0.41. So a scan whose far hits fit under 0.6 is refused
(`change_watch.MAP_FIT_MIN`) and counted as `off_map`. Reproduced first: both
days' recordings replayed in order through the deployed watch logged the three
bedroom reports as the rover had; with the gate, none, and the armchairs,
2026-10-10's armchair and controls 1 to 6 came out as before, under 0.5% of
their scans refused. Deployed at 14:48 and the bedroom revisited at 14:52 with
the pose holding (3 cm): nothing logged and nothing refused. The bedroom cells
counted at the bad pose were cleared from the watch's state first, its file kept
as `7da19bef3888.npz.before-bedroom-purge`.

Why the pose went out entering the bedroom is navigation's question and is not
answered here.

## The blind spot: a move the rover watched

The rover stood in the living room from 12:51 to 14:12 and the owner moved the
armchairs about 13:10. The watch keeps one visit per place while it is in view,
so one visit took in both arrangements, the chairs there for a quarter of it;
it closed blurred when the rover left, in place of the visit that had seen
them, and where they had stood was never reported gone. The rover spends most
of a day in that room, so this is the common case, not a corner.

A fix was tried: a visit also closing after ten minutes, a blurred visit not
replacing a clear reference, and a visit seen from one spot not replacing one at
all. It made a test of the scene pass, and on replay brought back the wall from
control 3 and three of the bedroom reports, even with the map gate: keeping an
old reference keeps one recorded at a bad pose (control 1 had 11 s at a fit of
0.74 a metre from that wall), which replacing it with each visit had been
quietly healing. Reverted; it needs a reference that heals and still remembers.
