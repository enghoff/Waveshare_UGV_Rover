# M5 session 7: version 4 made no wrong merge on the session or in any of its five draws, followed four of seven trials, and passes

**Version 4 of [the world model kept per visit](../plans/a-world-model-kept-per-visit.md)
passed its test.** With session 7 added, no record took a thing it did not
already hold, in any of the seven sessions, on the run with every picture or in
any of five draws each without a random 1% of the unlabelled pictures, as
declared before the session
([the plan](../../experiments/m5_changes/staged/session7-planned.json)). The black
bean bag was set down 0.25 m from where the red one had stood a minute before,
and version 4 kept each in its own record. On the same pictures the deployed
store made 20 wrong merges over the seven sessions, version 1 10, versions 2
and 3 2 each. [R-WS-17](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-17) stays proposed:
acceptance is the shadow run on the rover the plan names next.

## The session

`session7-1017`, as planned in
`experiments/m5_changes/staged/session7-planned.json`, except for its start:

- **A manual drive before it.** With the black bean bag, the stool and the
  step ladder set out but the red bean bag not yet, the rover was driven by hand
  from the charger to the bedroom and back (10:38-10:43), lost its position in
  the living room for three minutes and parked on a weak fit. Its pictures count
  as part of the session (they were labelled with the rest); the three minutes
  without a sound pose are a bad-pose window, left out of scoring as before.
- **A refit.** After the reboot navigation would not settle (70% of the scan on
  a wall; a 20-degree turn brought 80%). The owner refitted the rover in the
  bedroom and the session ran from there, unplugged: loop A from the bedroom
  end 11:39-11:43, the wait at bedroom-2 for 85 s while the owner made the moves,
  loop B, a 3.5-minute run, home at 11:53, 8 cm from the spot. Every stop's
  position check stayed within 6 cm; the battery went from 84% to 64%.

The moves, read from the session's labelled photographs: the red bean bag 2.0 m
round the table's corridor end; the black bean bag 2.8 m from the gap by wall A
to 0.25 m from where the red one had stood; the stool 1.8 m back beside the
cabinet. The step ladder stayed.

The change watch's final count failed on a line of zero bytes the power cut had
left in its log; the reader now skips such lines, and the count was rerun.

## What it means

The margin that version 4 puts on every bar costs filed pictures (79% on the
session against 91-93%) and leaves more of them unfiled rather than wrongly
filed, which is what the rule asks. One session and five draws are not proof
that it never mixes two things, which is why the plan's next step is the shadow
run: version 4 kept beside the deployed store from the rover's own looks,
reporting only, with honesty shown by its ordinary drives.
