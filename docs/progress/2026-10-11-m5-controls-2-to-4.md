# M5 controls 2 to 4: one false alarm, from a pose a little wrong, and the watch now wants two viewpoints

**Three more control runs on 2026-10-11, run unattended at the owner's word,
logged one furniture-sized change, and it was false.** In control 3 the rover
stood 27 s doing a look with its pose 10 cm and 3 degrees out, and beams running
almost along a hall wall passed through it: 0.7 m of wall was logged as gone.
The change watch now reports a change only once the scans that make it one came
from places at least 0.5 m apart (686f989, b48562e). Replayed through the fixed
watch, yesterday's armchair move is still found and today's four controls report
nothing of furniture size. By the rule proposed this morning
([three clean controls](../decisions/m5-moves-on-after-three-clean-controls.md),
not yet agreed) the count starts again on the fix.

## The false alarm

At 09:16:45 the watch logged a GONE 0.70 m across at (-10.04, -10.83), a stretch
of the hall wall with a room behind it never seen, last seen solid in control 1
at 08:09 from 0.6 m away. Read off control 3's recording: from 09:16:14 to
09:16:41 the rover stood at (-9.77, -12.86) for a look, and only 73% of each
scan lay within a cell of the map's walls, against 92-100% before and after;
at 09:16:44 the mapper moved the pose 10 cm and 3 degrees and it read 100%. In
those 27 s, 5.2 beams a scan passed through the wall's cells, running almost
along it; after the correction, 0.3. No beam reached the room behind, so it was
not a door opened.

The watch's own fit check did not catch it. It allows 10 cm for the pose, and
judged against what the watch knows those scans fitted at a median of 95%
against 98% for all the others, so no threshold on it separates them without
refusing a third of good scans. Fitted against the mapper's map they do separate
(73% against 92-100%), but a gate there at 85% cut 10 to 25% of each run's scans.
What does separate them is where they came from: every scan that made the wall
"gone" was taken within 0.30 m, while yesterday's armchair was seen gone from
places 5.8 m apart and back from 3.9 m apart. So a group is reported once its
evidence spans 20 s and 0.5 m of viewpoint (`change_watch.VIEW_SPREAD_M`): from
one spot a change is a reason to look again, which is what the plan proposed.

Reproduced first: controls 1 to 3 replayed through the deployed watch logged
the wall, as the rover had. On the fix, replayed in order, sessions 15-16, 17
and 20 of 2026-10-10 and controls 1 to 4 report the armchair's return, gone
from where it stood and back in its place, and nothing else of furniture size.
Session 18-19's recording could not be read on the rover and was left out. The
first deploy of the fix loaded the watch's saved file, which predates the
viewpoints, and treated unknown as seen from everywhere: the wall was back in
the report. Reproduced in `test_change_watch` and fixed (b48562e); deployed,
the report is empty and the 35 checks pass on the rover.

The rule also means a change seen only from the charger, where the rover
stands most of the day, is not reported until a run sees it from elsewhere.
