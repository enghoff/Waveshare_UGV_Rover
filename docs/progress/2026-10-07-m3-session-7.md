## The battery

The reading 1.5 s after the stop was 10%, and I took it for the battery at rest.
It was the voltage still recovering from load. Five seconds later it read 35%,
and the owner saw 40%. `watch_run.py` already waits for three readings 5 s apart
with the rover standing, so the watcher would not have made this mistake; a
single reading straight after a stop is not one at rest. The owner's floor for
supervised drives is now 10% at rest.
