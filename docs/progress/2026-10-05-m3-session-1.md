# M3 session 1: two runs, neither ended by going home, and one stuck beside a wall

**The first supervised M3 session ran two runs for 8.7 minutes and 27 m, and neither
reached the ending R-AUT-13 asks for.** The first stopped on its 40-action limit
after 3.7 minutes, which was my mistake in setting it up. The second stopped after
three failures in a row. A look had left the rover close beside a wall, and
navigation refused every drive until it turned or backed up — which a run is not
permitted to do. By hand, one 46-degree turn freed it. The fixes of 2026-10-03
held: no fruitless goal was retried, and no refused place was driven at again.
[R-AUT-13](../requirements/autonomy.md#r-aut-13) stays open;
[R-SAFE-16](../requirements/safety.md#r-safe-16) held. The owner was in the room
for both runs, and nothing in the record shows contact.

## Before the runs

After a boot the map was not settled. The rover was parked by the charger at an
oblique angle to a wall, so its scan fitted the map two ways almost equally (97%
against 96%), and every drive would have been refused at dispatch. The owner turned
it to face the room. A refit then scored 98% against a 77% rival and confirmed the
pose without moving it. Battery was 75%.

## The runs

| | Run 1 (`run/c2258a72/1`) | Run 2 (`run/c2258a72/2`) |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | 900 s, standing limits (40 actions) | 600 s, no action limit |
| Lasted | 223 s, 10.7 m, 40 actions | 301 s, 16.4 m, 37 actions |
| Geometry looks | 16, two improved a placement | 14, one improved, two made one worse |
| Failures | two viewpoints refused before moving | one timeout, one refused viewpoint, then two refusals beside a wall |
| Ended | action limit, mid-drive | three failures in a row |

Run 1's two improvements were `object:404` (0.33 m better, to 0.69 m) and
`object:353` (0.46 m better, to 0.34 m). Every other look left its thing no better,
and the thing was put aside after that one goal, as the fix of 2026-10-03 intends.
The two viewpoints navigation refused were places the rover's body does not fit,
and neither was chosen again. Forty actions are about four minutes of this
executive's work, so the standing limit is too small for a session. The rover was
driven back to the start by hand before run 2.

Run 2 went further: out to 4 m from the start, then back past it. One look improved
`object:463` by 0.34 m, to 0.48 m. Two made a placement worse. `object:380` went from
0.51 m to 1.42 m, and `object:253` from 0.96 m to 1.08 m. Two things put aside earlier
came back. That is as designed: a thing is put aside for 15 minutes or until its
placement changes, and both had changed.

## How run 2 ended

A goal at (-15.7, -13.4) left the rover facing a wall, with the wall 0.2 m to its
left. Navigation then refused the next two drives with the same reason: "the rover
is standing inside something the costmap believes in -- turn on the spot or back up,
then try again". The executive chose a different goal each time, and each was
refused for the same reason. The third failure ended the run, exactly as R-SAFE-16
requires. A run may use only `drive_to`, `world_inspect` and `stop`
(`rover_daemon/permission.py`). So it could not have taken the advice even if it
had tried, and nothing in the executive recognises the reason. By hand,
`turn_in_place` by -45 degrees, then a `drive_to`, returned it 2.9 m to the charger.

## What it means

R-AUT-13 has still not been shown, because no run has yet run out of things to do.
That needs a run that cannot end on a limit or on being stuck. Two things stand in
the way, both small:

- **A run must be able to get itself out of the costmap.** Either admit a bounded
  turn on the spot as a fourth operation, or have the daemon's `drive_to` make the
  turn itself when the start is inside the costmap. The refusal and the hand
  recovery are in this session's record, so the fault can be reproduced in a test
  before either is built.
- **Sessions should open with no action limit**, as the runbook allows; time,
  travel and failures still bound them.

Battery fell from 75% to 35% over the session's twenty minutes, about ten of them
driving. That fits the 20 to 25 minutes of this driving a charge was measured to
give before. M3 asks for twenty sessions and 120 minutes. At about nine minutes
a session, the twenty come to three hours, so the count, not the time, is what
binds, at one or two sessions a charge.
