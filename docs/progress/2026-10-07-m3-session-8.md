# M3 session 8: near goals no longer swing, and the run found its way home

**Run `run/58d585a2/3` drove the charger room, fenced, for about 9 minutes and
ended by going home.** It took 52 goals, 103 actions and 32.6 m, and stopped
8 cm from where it began. This was the first session after
[the near-goal fix](2026-10-07-why-the-rover-turns-back-and-forth-on-the-spot.md).
None of its 25 short goals took more than 4.6 s. In
[session 7](2026-10-07-m3-session-7.md), 3 took over 10 s and one swung for
72 s. It is also the first sighting of session 6's way home past the 0.6 m
margin: the fence one tenth of a metre wider let the drive back through. There
was no contact, and the rover stayed inside the safe area.

## What happened

- **Opened** 11:38 at the charger, (-17.29, -15.90), 65% charge. The safe area
  was the charger room, with no action or travel limit.
- **Navigation goals.** There were 30 in all. Of the 25 under 1.5 m, the median
  took 2.4 s and the longest 4.6 s; session 7's median was 1.4 s and its longest
  72.2 s. Near goals with a heading now arrive as a turn, a straight line and a
  turn: 59 turns in the session. No drive went 20 s without moving 0.3 m.
- **Two turns ran out of time** on their 8 s allowance. One, 73 degrees, was
  beside something: its goal had been moved 14 cm off an obstacle. That drive
  was reported as failed (`code 701`) and the run went on.
- **Three drives failed in all.** One was that turn. One goal, moved onto
  floor where the body fits, reached the safe-area margin. One started from
  inside the costmap's band. None came in a row.
- **48 looks; one improved its thing**: object:300 went from 1.28 m to 0.52 m.
  The rest were no better, which is the M4 blocker
  ([2026-10-06](2026-10-06-looks-seldom-reach-their-thing.md)).
- **Ended** 11:46:58: "there was nothing left worth doing", then "it went back to
  where the run started". Stopped at (-17.34, -15.84) against a start of
  (-17.29, -15.90). That is [R-AUT-13](../requirements/autonomy.md#r-aut-13)'s
  ending, seen a second time. 30% at rest afterwards.

## The battery alert was wrong

A live alert script polled every 2 s and called the floor after three readings
of 10% or less while not driving. It fired at 11:44:22, eight seconds after a
reading of 50%. Read again straight away, the battery stood at 40% (11.38 V).
A turn sags the voltage for a moment, and three 2-s readings fit inside one.
The script now needs every reading over 12 s of standing to be at the floor
(`experiments/m3_trials/session_alerts.py`). The run was not touched.

## What it means for M3

- The charger room has now been driven with every fix it found, so its
  condition is met.
- Two fixes are seen working on the rover: near goals no longer swing, and the
  way home is no longer refused by the margin.
- **A near goal's turns are new ways for a drive to fail.** A turn beside an
  obstacle can run out of time where a single Nav2 goal would have looped round
  instead. One in 59 here. It is worth watching in the conditions still ahead,
  especially moved furniture.
