# M3 session 13: the rover could not see a person in its way until it had set off

**Two runs in the charger room, about 5 minutes and 12 m, from 75%; the owner
blocked it twice.** The first time it waited and then drove on. The second
time it had already set off on a goal 1.0 m away: it swung on the spot for
25 s until the stall watch ended the goal. The cause is that a near goal
checked its way against the planner's map, which is slam_toolbox's map alone
and never has a person standing in front of a still rover on it. A near goal
now checks the live scan before it sets off and every second while it drives
(91319d2, deployed that evening). The person condition is still owed until a
session shows it. The first run also ended wedged at the edge of the rug under the
dining table. A drive target near the rug now counts as on it (90a522b,
deployed).

## What happened

- **First run**, `run/58d585a2/13`: 2.2 minutes, 6.5 m, 17 actions. At
  15:57:51 the owner stood in the way of a near goal. The rover held still for
  3 s, then drove on in one straight line. What set off the wait was the
  planner's route bending round, and the planner's map holds a person only by
  chance, so whether it was waiting for the owner is not certain.
  The run then chose four spots 3-23 cm outside the rug's box, among the
  chair legs, and drove onto the rug. Three drives in a row failed because
  navigation held the rover to be inside an obstacle, and the run ended.
  Neither a drive back to the start nor a straight 0.5 m forward could free it
  (the second stopped after 10 cm for something in its way). The owner lifted
  it out.
- **Second run**, `run/58d585a2/14`: 2.5 minutes, 5.6 m, 25 actions. The
  owner stepped in front of a goal 1.0 m away just after it set off. Nav2's
  controller would not drive into them and turned back and forth on the spot,
  replanning every second. The planner kept the straight line through them.
  The stall watch ended the goal after 25 s. I stopped the run to fix it, and
  the owner took the rover to charge.

## The changes

- **A near goal looks for what is in its way on the live scan**
  (`ros_nav/nav_moves.py`, 91319d2). Before setting off, and every second while
  driving, it checks the straight line to the goal against the local costmap,
  the one costmap the scan's obstacles are on. Blocked, it stops, holds still
  and checks every second, and drives on once the way clears. After 3 s it goes
  round: the shortest way over the local costmap that keeps its centre 0.3 m
  from whatever the scan sees, cut into at most four straight legs
  (`goal_fit.legs_round`). With no such way, or after three stops, the goal is
  handed back as blocked. `test_way_round.py` replays this session's geometry
  with a planner that cannot see the owner. On the old code it fails as the
  rover did. ros_nav 615 tests pass. Deployed after the rover's reboot on the
  charger, and checked there: the bridge's service for the live costmap
  answers, in the odom frame, 3 m square; the straight-line check finds the
  wall 0.53 m to the rover's left and the obstacle 0.63 m ahead of it at the
  charger, and leaves the open side clear; a way-round search takes 0.02 s.
- **A drive target counts as on the rug when the body would reach it**
  (`autonomy/places.py`, 90a522b): a marked area refuses goals within 0.25 m
  of it, the footprint's furthest corner. Deployed and checked on the rover:
  all four of this session's spots are refused, and the origin is not.
  Autonomy 797 tests pass.

## What it means for M3

The person condition stays open until a session shows the deployed change
holding still for a person who steps in front, and going round one who stays.
