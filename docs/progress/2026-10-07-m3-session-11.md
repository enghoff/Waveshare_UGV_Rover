# M3 session 11: blocked by a person, the rover swung on the spot; now it waits

**The owner stood in the rover's path at about half a metre, twice.** It did
not drive into them. Both times it turned back and forth on the spot, which the
owner called erratic and unnecessary. The first time it went on once they moved.
The second time the stall watch ended the drive after 25 s, and the run recorded
a failed goal and carried on. The cause is the session 7 swing in another form:
with a person in the way, a goal 0.7 m off planned as a 3.6 m route round them.
A near goal now waits, still, for the way to clear. The person condition was
tried but found a fault, so it is owed again with the fix.

## What happened

- **First run**, `run/58d585a2/7`: 3.3 minutes, 10.2 m, 32 actions, from the
  origin at 20%. The owner's first block: back and forth, then on to the goal.
  One look was refused because the rover's own look had been running 11 s, past
  the 9 s the daemon waits for it. That counted as a failed goal.
- **The floor**, applied the owner's way. It tripped at 11.04 V. After a
  minute's rest the reading was back at 15% (11.13 V), so the run was reopened.
- **Second run**, `run/58d585a2/8`: 1.3 minutes, 1.9 m, 12 actions. The owner's
  second block. Navigation ended the drive itself: "it has not got 0.5 m further
  on in 25 seconds and Nav2 is not recovering ... the route round was 3.6 m for a
  goal 0.7 m away". The floor tripped again, and after a minute's rest the
  reading was 10% (11.06 V). It drove 1.6 m back and ended 4 cm from the origin.
- **The owner's note:** the drives are short hops, hard to step in front of in
  time.

## The changes

- **A near goal waits when its straight way is blocked** (`ros_nav/nav_moves.py`,
  `wait_for_the_way`). After turning to face the goal, the rover asks the planner
  for the route. If it is more than 0.5 m longer than the straight line, the
  rover holds still and asks again every 2 s. It drives once the route is
  straight. If after 10 s it is still not, the goal is handed back as "something
  is in the way", without being driven round. A stop ends the wait.
- **A run's look refused as busy is asked again** (`autonomy/executive.py`): up
  to twice, 3 s apart, each as a new recorded dispatch.
- ros_nav 592 and autonomy 791 passed here. Each new test fails on the old code:
  a blocked goal was driven round, and the busy look ended its goal
  "interrupted".

## What it means for M3

The person condition stays open until a session shows the rover holding still
for a person and going on when they move. Next time, the easiest way to block
it is to step in front as it turns to face its next spot.
