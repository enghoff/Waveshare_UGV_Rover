# M5 session 8: the world model made no wrong merge on pictures it had not seen, and the run looked at every change first and none twice, so every M5 criterion reads as met

**On session 8 the world model kept per visit, filing live on the rover,
put no picture of one thing in another thing's record, in the session or in
any of the eight sessions before it.** That was the count M5 failed on. The
red bean bag was set down 0.08 m from where the black one stood the day
before, the case that failed version 2 in session 5, and its record followed
it through the move. The autonomous run's first four choices were the four
furniture-sized changes the change watch reported, ahead of any record, and
it chose none of them twice. Every M5 criterion now reads as met; M5 passes
when the owner signs it off.

[R-WS-18](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-18) stay proposed: their
acceptance asks for labels by someone other than the code's author, against
