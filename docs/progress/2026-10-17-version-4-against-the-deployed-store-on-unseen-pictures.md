# Version 4 against the deployed store on pictures neither was tuned on: better on every measure

**On M5 session 7 and three ordinary drives, the only pictures version 4 of
[the world model kept per visit](../plans/a-world-model-kept-per-visit.md) was
not developed on, it beats the deployed store on every measure.** It mixed no
two things where the store mixed four, kept unmoved things together where the
store scattered them, filed more of the labelled pictures and followed more of
session 7's moves. The owner asked on 2026-10-17 for the choice between the two
to be made on which performs better, rather than on fixed targets; on these
pictures there is a clear winner. [R-WS-17](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-17)
stays proposed: moving it needs labels by someone other than the coding agent.

## The drives

`drives1-1017`, 12:33-12:47: the survey loop three times running from the
charger, then home, run by the coding agent with nothing staged. The four
things of session 7 had been cleared away before them. 24 stops, every
position check within 6.3 cm and 1.5 degrees; 86% to 64% at rest. A child sat
on the bed during the first loop. The eleven unmoved objects were labelled from
the photographs before any model ran on them (`cbe4be4` on `visit-model`), and
the drives' lidar recording was added to the runs (the scripts had looked only
for recordings named for sessions).

## The comparison

Both scored on the same pictures by the agreed counts
([the rule](../decisions/the-world-model-is-pass-fail-on-honesty-alone.md)).

| | Deployed store | Version 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong merges, session 7 and the drives | 4 | 0 |
| Unmoved objects in two records or fewer | 6 of 22 | 21 of 22 |
| Labelled pictures filed, session 7 / drives | 71% / 79% | 79% / 89% |
| Session 7's trials kept, strict | 2 of 7 | 4 of 7 |

The deployed store's wrong merges on the drives were both paintings: one record
holding the snowy-tree, forest and village paintings, another the village
painting with the animals painting and the front door. Versions 1 to 3 made one
each on the drives.

In five draws without a random 1% of the unlabelled pictures, version 4 made no
wrong merge on session 7 or the drives. In one of them it made one in session
5: two far-off pictures of the snowy-tree painting, taken from the charger at
that session's start, in the blue painting's record, the place version 3 failed
most often. Over the twelve runs of version 4 on pictures it was not developed on, that is one wrong merge.

## What it means

Version 4 is the better of the two on everything measured, by margins no
single session decides: four wrong merges to none, six unmoved objects held
together to twenty-one. It is not perfect: the far-off dark painting is still a
place where it can, rarely, take one painting for another, and the deployed
store does so routinely. The plan's next step is to run version 4 on the rover
from the live looks and move the world state's consumers over to it one at a
time.
