# M3 session 10: the person condition was not really tried; the rover caught on a pair of shoes

**Two runs in the charger room, 5 minutes and 17.5 m in all, ended at the owner's
10% floor and drove back to the origin.** The condition was meant to be a person
moving through the area. By the owner's account they walked in view once, so it
was not exercised and stays open. The owner also saw the rover get stuck on a
pair of shoes once. That is a physical contact with something below the lidar's
plane: criterion 5's subject, and a reminder that the area was not fully
cleared. The owner adds that the rover often gets stuck on a rug, though not in
this session. No drive failed and navigation attempted no recovery in either
run.

## What happened

- **First run**, `run/58d585a2/5`: 3.5 minutes, 14.0 m, 42 actions, 21 looks,
  no failures, no placement improved. I stopped it at 11.35 V standing (35%) on
  a voltage floor I had made up after session 9's failed pivots. That was
  wrong: the owner's standing rule is 10% at rest and then back to the origin,
  and they said so.
- **Second run**, `run/58d585a2/6`: 1.5 minutes, 3.5 m, 15 actions, no
  failures. Stopped at 10% over 12 s standing (11.02 V). A person's drive took
  it 0.44 m back to the origin, ending 12 cm from it.
- **The shoes.** Nothing on the rover's side records them. The slowest goal of
  the session was 3.45 m in 20 s, about twice a clear drive's time, and may be
  it. The rover freed itself before the stall watch's 25 s.

## What it means for M3

- **The person condition is still owed.** It needs someone crossing the rover's
  path and standing where it is heading, several times over a session.
- **Criterion 5 counts contacts, and the lidar cannot see shoes or a rug.** It
  scans one plane about 20 cm up. A shoe left out is a clearing failure. A rug
  that is part of the room and often catches the tracks is a hazard the rover
  cannot sense, and it stays one until it is marked on the map as somewhere not
  to drive, or taken up for sessions. That is the owner's choice.
- **A stuck rover goes unrecorded unless it stays stuck for 25 s.** The run
  measures neither travel against command nor wheel slip, so short snags like
  this one leave no trace in the episode record.
