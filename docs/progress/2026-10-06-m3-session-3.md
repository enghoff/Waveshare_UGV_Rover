# M3 session 3: a run ran out of things to do, drove back to its start and ended

**The third supervised session showed R-AUT-13's ending on the rover.** Fenced
to the room the charger opens into, the run worked for 3.6 minutes and 15.6 m,
found nothing left worth doing, drove back to where it was opened and handed the
run in without latching. It came to rest 16 cm from its start: the start, beside
the charger, is too close to something for the body to stand in, so the goal was
moved 11 cm to where it fits. The
other half of the requirement, taking a goal whose only fault is the trip, had
already happened six times in sessions 1 and 2. So
[R-AUT-13](../requirements/autonomy.md#r-aut-13) is now settled.
[R-SAFE-16](../requirements/safety.md#r-safe-16) held. None of the 19 looks
improved a placement.

## Before the run

The rover was parked by the charger at (-17.03, -16.01), facing 148 degrees,
with its position trusted and the map settled. A refit offered a pose 6 cm and
8 degrees away, and the mapper kept its own. That is the disagreement the
morning's entity-evidence trial left after a fast turn, and it did not stop
anything here. Everything deployed matched main. The owner unplugged the rover
and handed it over, having been asked to clear the room and stay in it with the
console open. Battery was 70% off the charger.

## The run

`run/be1da2f4/1`, opened over the protocol at 10:33 with 1200 s, no travel or
action limit, three failures in a row, and a safe area of x -21.0 to -15.3 and
y -18.9 to -10.3: the room the charger opens into, 5.7 by 8.6 m, with its table.
The fence is what let it run out of things before the battery did. Session 2's
run had the whole flat and was still going when the battery reached 5%.

It lasted 217 s, 15.6 m and 39 actions over 21 turns (episodes 780 to 800):

- **19 geometry goals.** 16 reached their viewpoint and looked. Seven looks stored
  new observations. Nine were refused as "the same picture as the last look",
  usually the rover's own looking having just taken it on arrival. Not one goal
  left its thing better placed. Each was put aside for 15 minutes, as designed.
- **Three failed drives, never two in a row.** Two were viewpoints refused in
  2 s because the body does not fit within half a metre of them. That is the
  bridge's whole-body test at the goal, which the run's own walk over the map does
  not copy ([the pocket](2026-10-05-the-pocket-by-the-charger.md)). The third was
  refused as standing inside the costmap 12 s into a drive, part-way along a
  route, which is the case the back-off deliberately leaves alone, as in session 2.
  The next drive arrived.
- **No frontier goals.** The room is fully mapped.

## How it ended

The 20th turn weighed 64 candidates, all of them looks: 42 with nowhere the body
could reach to take them from, 20 put aside, and 2 at places a failed drive had
ruled out for half an hour. Nothing was held back by its trip alone, so there was
nothing further afield to take. The executive drove back to the start
(`return_to_start`, episode 800). The drive arrived after the goal was moved 11 cm
to where the body fits. The rover stopped at (-17.15, -15.90), facing 142 degrees,
and the run closed with "there was nothing left worth doing ... it went back to
where the run started". Autonomy was off and not latched. Battery was 55%.

## Going further, from the record

Decisions that took a goal because nothing nearer was worth doing carry "it costs
more than it gains, and is taken because nothing nearer is worth doing". The
rover's record has six: episode 733 in session 1, and 762, 771, 773, 774 and 776
in session 2, one of them a frontier. With this run's ending, both halves of
R-AUT-13 have now happened on the rover.

## What it means

M3 stands at three sessions and about 23 minutes of autonomy time, against 20
sessions and 120 minutes. A fenced room makes a short session. At this length the
count of sessions binds rather than the time, and several fit in a charge.

What the run did not do is improve anything. None of 19 looks did, against 1 in
37 in session 2 and 3 in 30 in session 1. I have not looked into why. Every
geometry goal here was chosen because it promised a better placement, and nine
of its looks stored nothing because a picture from the same spot already existed.
Those two facts are worth checking against each other before more sessions count
looks as work done.
