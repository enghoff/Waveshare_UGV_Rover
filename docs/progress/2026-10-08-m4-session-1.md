# M4 session 1: filing by aim on the rover -- 7 filings, all of the right kind of thing

**Three runs from a full power-up, the last unfenced over the whole flat, ended
on the battery and driven home. Aimed looks named their target for the first
time ([the case](../decisions/aimed-looks-file-to-their-target.md), agreed that
morning).** Of 44 aimed looks, 7 filed a region to their target, and each
photograph shows the target's kind of thing. Three ranged filings took a
target's claim to 0.20-0.24 m, and the goals measured that as gain: 4
goals improved their thing, against none in the first run's 9. The first run also
found that an aimed look was thrown away as "the same picture" as the one taken
on arrival, and that the run did not keep what filing said; both were fixed and
deployed between runs (5701ae0). R-WS-13 stays open.

## Before the session: a rover that could not feel itself turn

The owner reported that a requested turn moved the rover only a few degrees. The
board's motion sensor had frozen since the 08:07 start: every reading identical,
`gz` -28417, `az` 0, the gyro bias logged at -1,100 to +2,100 deg/s, so Nav2's
spin counted itself done within 0.1-0.3 s. This is the fault of
[2026-10-05](2026-10-05-frozen-imu.md) and
[2026-10-06](2026-10-06-a-rover-that-cannot-feel-itself-turn.md). The board's
own reboot command, `{"T":600}` over WiFi with the wheels still, did **not** clear
it: the readings were byte-identical afterwards. The owner's full power cycle
did; the bias read +0.397 deg/s two minutes after boot, and +0.37 to +0.40
through the session. Autonomous runs were already refused during such a fault
(R-SAFE-17); a turn asked for by hand is not.

What this shows and does not:

- **No filing put a region under the wrong kind of thing.** The refusals look
  right too where checked: object:464 is the owner, standing in the kitchen
  doorway on 2026-10-07; the region at the aim was the empty doorway, and
  appearance refused it.
- **A claim from one range can be over-confident on a big or mixed record.**
  object:367's first filing ranged the drawers and claimed 0.24 m; the second
  ranged the cabinet door about a metre along the counter, and the claim went
  back to 0.62. The two chair records ended claiming 0.20 m for a single chair
  and for the table's group of chairs.
- **Same-object suspects were named and cooled, but missed the rug.** object:461
  named one suspect and the two chair filings named seven and eight, each
  including the other chair records. The rug has at least two records, 338 and
  417, and neither named the other, so the run looked at the rug twice by way of
  two records -- the return the owner asked about. Run 3 also went from the
  single chair (457) to the group of chairs (637) a minute later; 637 was not
  among 457's suspects.
