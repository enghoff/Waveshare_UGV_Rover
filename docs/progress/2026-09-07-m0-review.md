# M0 review: bounded calibration and usable operating limits

Status: P0 is in progress; M0 has not passed. This is a review of existing evidence
and the agreed scope, not a new physical measurement or a deployed calibration fix.
The current protocol and acceptance gate are in
[the autonomy plan](../plans/autonomous-curiosity.md#phase-0----close-the-semantic-world-state-prerequisite).

## Evidence available at this review

The [September 7 baseline](2026-09-07-m0-semantic-world-state.md) preserves the driven
recording, reviewed associations and subsequent camera-to-camera bench findings.
Its original criterion counts describe the gate used at that time; they do not
certify the current M0 criteria.

- Range-carrying replay was repaired. The recorded comparison reports 97 entities
  with ranges and 100 without, with two reviewed false crossings rejected by range.
  That supports the mechanism, not reliable range attribution in every case.
- The latest offline suite run during this review sequence, using
  `python world_state/selftest.py` at repository commit `3040311`, passed **748 checks,
  with 0 failures and 0 skipped**. This was local software verification; it did not
  measure hardware pointing or depth accuracy.
- Paired approaches in the reported bench produced yaw differences of 1.58, 1.58
  and 1.32 degrees at commanded pan -20, 0 and +20 respectively. Same-direction
  fit variation was 0.03-0.05 degrees. This is strong evidence of a repeatable
  direction-dependent effect at those tested positions; it is not a full-travel
  guarantee or the residual error after compensation.
- The gain-like slope was reported as 3.7-7.6%, with drift limiting precision.
  A single 7% correction is not established. Pan-dependent roll remains observed,
  with lens/axis/servo contributions not independently separated.
- Lower-appearance-score wrong associations and floor patches treated as objects
  remain in the baseline. The review does not close their movement-eligibility gates.
- The separately recorded [refit investigation](2026-09-07-refit-window.md) reports
  34 unplaced observations stamped before pose confirmation, from a heading later
  corrected by 152.5 degrees. It identifies the missing capture gate in R-WS-16.
  This review did not repeat that hardware experiment. Stationary independent
  camera calibration can proceed, but driven M0 acceptance also needs that gate
  and a way to keep the affected bearings out of association without losing images.
- The owner printed the independent A4 ChArUco reference and reported all five
  dimensional checks spot on on 2026-09-07: the 100 mm bar, 240 x 168 mm board and
  both 120 mm five-square spans. The print-scale gate therefore passes. Flatness,
  camera detection and pose accuracy remain to be established after mounting.

## Limits of the measurement chain

[The lens constants](../../face_tracking/lens.py) were derived from pan/tilt sweeps
using commanded angles. [The OAK bench](../../world_state/bench_oak.py) uses that
lens and reads back the angle commanded to the servo, not an independently sensed
shaft position. Repeating that fit can reveal disagreement and repeatability, but
cannot by itself establish a unique servo correction independent of lens error.

The configured 1.5-degree bearing uncertainty is an assumption to validate, not a
required mechanical accuracy. A separation between approach directions is neither
automatically a standard deviation nor the unavoidable error of a corrected system.
Measurement uncertainty, repeatable bias and remaining physical variation need
separate estimates before any uncertainty setting changes.

## Scope agreed following the review

No requirement changes state in this review. R-WS-10 remains failing; R-WS-11,
R-WS-12, R-WS-13 and R-WS-16 remain open. The software suite is not evidence that any of
these physical/acceptance requirements has been settled.

The owner explicitly asked that calibration respect the physical limitations of
the setup and not pursue unattainable precision. The agreed M0 interpretation is
adequate measured accuracy and honest uncertainty within declared useful limits,
with unsupported conditions refused. Perfect pointing and identifying every object
are not acceptance requirements.

The plan now specifies a bounded campaign, held-out task checks and exit decisions:
accept a useful demonstrated envelope, narrow it and retest, or stop with the
remaining limitation documented. No operating envelope has yet been accepted and
no calibration constant is changed by this review.

The old baseline's "before Phase 1" heading is historical. Read-only M1 recording
and M2 shadow decisions may proceed alongside P0; semantic movement still waits for
M0 and the subsequent execution gates. The reference scale is verified; mounting,
detection and the first independent pose measurements are the next P0 steps.
