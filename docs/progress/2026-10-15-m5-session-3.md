# M5 session 3: moving a record made no difference, and the drive home was lost to half a second the board reader missed

**The staged test of a record following its moved thing does not pass: with the
step and without it the session counts the same.** Of three moved things, the
armchair with the throw and the red bean bag kept their records and the cabinet
split, and no record took two things. The step was asked once, about the
armchair, and declined. It did not need to act: an older record of the same
armchair already stood where it arrived.
[R-WS-17](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-17) stays proposed.

**The rover was lost two metres from the charger, and the cause was not the
gyro.** In the middle of a fast recovery spin on the way home, the daemon's
board reader went half a second unread. That gap was thrown away as a hole, and
with it 30 degrees of turn, which is more than the mapper's search can put back.
The gap is now integrated when its lines arrive, and that is deployed. The live
map took duplicated chair legs and was put back from the snapshot taken before
staging.

## Navigation

The session's recording, played from its starting graph into a second mapper
on its own ROS domain, followed the live mapper within a few centimetres and
went wrong in the same two places (`experiments/nav_replay`). That reproduces
the fault without the rover, as [R-NAV-11](../requirements/navigation.md#r-nav-11)
asks.

- **08:07:03-08:07:43, loop B in the living room:** the pose was up to 0.5 m
  out, by the session's own stop check and by the scans scored against the
  starting map. Fits there were poor everywhere, 0.45-0.65 among the moved
  furniture, and the pose came back by itself. The duplicated chair legs date
  from here. The cause is not settled.
- **08:10:58, the drive home:** the cause is known.
  - In a recovery spin at 60 deg/s, odometry stood still for 0.48 s while the
    lidar turned through about 30 degrees.
  - The board link's count of holes since the daemon started read 1.
  - The reader had been starved, the gap passed `MAX_SAMPLE_GAP_S` (0.5 s), and
    the turn in it was dropped.
  - Every other turn of the session agreed with the lidar to within a few
    degrees. This includes the owner's drive back, and it was the first drive
    on the [gyro check](2026-10-14-a-gyro-that-turned-a-parked-rover.md). That
    check never fired.

The map with the duplicated legs was saved at 08:11:52 and again at 08:20.
That was correct by the keeper's rules: the restore had been confirmed and the
gyro was fine. A map-only restore from the snapshot taken before staging timed
out at 30 s while the mapper went on loading it. The live map then matched the
snapshot's walls in 8,385 of 8,386 cells, and the keeper saved it at 08:31.
The owner refitted the rover: 0.0 degrees and 3 cm from the lidar's fit.

## Fixed and deployed

`152fad1`, with ros_nav, rover_daemon and drive_web. Each fix was reproduced in
a test before the change.

- **A stalled reader is a hole only when lines are missing.** A gap is
  integrated when the lines that arrive for it cover at least half of it, at
  20 a second. Today's code counted a 0.6 s stall that delivered all twelve of
  its lines as a hole.
- **A graph load gets 90 s and is never asked for twice.**
  - The 190 MB graph took 38 s to load in the second mapper.
  - The 30-second limit is what failed the morning's restore. It also made the
    boot of 2026-10-14 ask for the graph 23 times while the mapper grew to
    4.2 GB.
  - The daemon's refit and restore limits and the console's snapshot wait move
    with it.
  - After the deploy, the map came back settled on its first load.
- **A refit that finds the rover already in place writes its pose to disk.**
  This went out with `64b01e8` before the session. On the rover the owner's
  refit wrote 143.4 degrees, where a stale 108 had made the previous restart
  come back unsettled.

Suites: ros_nav 827 on the rover at the deployed commit; rover_daemon 1250 and drive_web 654 here.
