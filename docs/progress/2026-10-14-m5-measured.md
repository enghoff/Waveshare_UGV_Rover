# M5 measured: the rover found 9 of 16 staged changes with no false alarm that held up, and goes to look at what changed, but fails on keeping each moved thing's identity

**M5's acceptance set is complete:**
- two counted sessions on 2026-10-14;
- the replay of 2026-10-10 that the owner chose in place of a third
  ([the amendment](../decisions/m5-sessions-are-counted-before-they-start.md#amended-2026-10-14-a-replay-in-place-of-the-third-session)).

**By [the milestone's criteria](../plans/autonomous-curiosity.md#milestone-m5-the-rover-notices-useful-changes-without-filling-the-world-with-duplicates):**
- **Detection:** the lidar change watch found 9 of 16 staged events, 56% (95%:
  33-77%), and 2 more small.
- **False alarms:** none held up in 20 room-controls, under the budget of one
  in seven.
- **Looking:** the rover goes to look at reported changes first, and leaves
  each alone once looked at.
- **Identity fails.** One record took both black armchairs, a confident wrong
  merge, which fails the criterion outright.

**M5 does not pass**, on criterion 4 alone. The multi-day benchmark is not run.

## Criterion by criterion

1. **The set is recorded:** the sessions' recordings, records and staging
   files, the replay's extracts and the store backups. Twenty room-controls
   tell a held-up rate under 14% from one above it, which is what the budget
   needs.
2. **Detection, reported with its interval: 9 of 16, 56% (33-77%)**, and 2
   more found small. What the watch finds and what it misses is clear.
   - **Found:** armchairs and bean bags, things solid at the lidar's height.
   - **Missed or small:** a dining chair, a step ladder, a box, a stool, and
     where a cabinet on legs had stood. Session 1 found five of seven, session
     2, staged with these, two.
3. **False alarms: none held up in 20 room-controls**, so the rate is under
   14% (95%). That is the budget of one in seven. Session 2's corridor logged
   one furniture-sized change. It was a door someone in the house opened during
   the run, and by the decision's rule a change shown to be a door is not a
   false alarm. The rate is not shown under one in ten.
4. **Identity fails.** Session 1 had no applicable trial: neither armchair had
   two pictures filed before the staging. And record `object:379` took
   pictures of both black armchairs as ordinary matches, which fails the
   criterion outright, as record 838 did on the shakedown. Session 2's identity
   was not counted; the failure already stands. The remedy is the world
   state's ([R-WS-17](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-17),
   [R-WS-18](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-18)) and is not built.
5. **A changed place outranks a confirmed one.** The acceptance scenarios show
   it (`autonomy/scenarios/changes.json`, criterion 5 drawn at 0.49 against
   0.01, [2026-10-11](2026-10-11-m5-investigate-change.md)). Both sessions'
   runs did it:
   - **Session 1:** the run started in the living room and chose to look at
     all five reported changes in its first 75 seconds.
   - **Session 2:** the run started from the bedroom, where it had waited,
     spent its first two and a half minutes on placements there, and on
     reaching the living room looked at both furniture-sized arrivals first.
6. **Refreshed places lose their pull.** In neither run was a change chosen
   again once looked at. That is the scenarios' criterion 6, a looked-at
   change worth nothing, shown on the floor.

## The benchmark's arm comparison

The proposed minimum improvement was that the autonomous arm looks at more
staged changes than the loop, finding no fewer and claiming no more false
ones. The loop never looks; the runs looked at five changes in session 1 and
two in session 2. In both sessions the run came second to a loop, or first
from the bedroom, so finding is not compared fairly between arms here. The
multi-day benchmark, three separate days with a restart and a map reset, has
not been run.

## What it means

The rover notices furniture-sized changes reliably and rarely cries wolf. It
misses thin, low and legged things, and its memory of things does not survive
a move. The camera-based step 3 of
[prediction in what and where streams](../plans/what-where-streams.md) found
three of session 2's misses but failed elsewhere. Next:
- the heading check that lets the camera place what it sees in a rearranged
  room, which both the identity work and step 3 need;
- then identity.

No requirement moved: R-WS-17 and R-WS-18 stay open, now with two sessions'
failures as their cases.
