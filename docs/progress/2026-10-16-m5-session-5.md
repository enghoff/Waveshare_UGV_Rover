# M5 session 5: version 2 followed the red bean bag through its move, but took the black bean bag for yesterday's red one, and fails

**Version 2 of [the world model kept per visit](../plans/a-world-model-kept-per-visit.md)
fails its first session it had never seen, on honesty, the one thing that
passes or fails it ([the rule](../decisions/the-world-model-is-pass-fail-on-honesty-alone.md)).**
The black bean bag, carried in for the session, was filed 18 times to the
record that held 21 pictures of the red bean bag from sessions 3 and 4. Version
1 did the same, and the deployed store mixed the two bean bags in five
records. Counted the same way, across sessions, the deployed store had
already failed M5's criterion 4 in session 1, which corrects this morning's
recount (below). [R-WS-17](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-17) stays proposed.

**What it did well is new.** Version 2 kept the red bean bag in one record
through its 3.5 m move, all ten of the session's pictures of it, where version 1
and the deployed store split it. No system had followed a moved thing within a
session before. By the strict count across sessions it does not count, because
older pictures of the bean bag sit in other records.

## A correction: M5's criterion 4 was not met

Counted across sessions, the deployed store already held two distinguishable
things in one record before today. The recount of 2026-10-16 morning, on which
the owner signed M5 off ([the armchairs' decision](../decisions/the-two-armchairs-cannot-be-told-apart.md)),
compared pictures within each session only.

- **`object:914`** took three pictures of the black bean bag in the shakedown
  (one as an ordinary match), then one of the red bean bag in session 1 as an
  ordinary match.
- **`object:930`** took the black bean bag in the shakedown, then the red bean
  bag in session 1 (two ordinary matches), session 3 and session 4.
- In session 1 the red bean bag was put down where the black one had stood in
  the shakedown: the same swap that version 2 failed on today.

Both are ordinary matches during an acceptance session, which fails criterion
4 outright whatever is decided about the armchairs. Two more of the deployed
store's records hold two paintings each (`object:595` on the shakedown,
`object:444` in session 1), outside criterion 4's labelled things. Version 2,
counted the same way over sessions 1 to 4, still holds no record of two
distinguishable things; version 1 holds two (a painting with an armchair, and
two paintings).

## Why the black bean bag went to the red one's record

- **Nothing had said the red bean bag was gone.** The rover made no recorded
  drive between session 4 and this one, so its record still stood where the
  bean bag had been put down yesterday.
- **The black bean bag stood 0.6 m from that place**, close enough to be "here
  as before".
- **The colour veto did not stop it, and why is not yet known.** The two bags'
  colours are far apart on this session's pictures: at most 0.27 between them,
  against a median of 0.75 for the black bag with itself. The veto compares a
  region with the best of a record's colour samples, so one dark sample
  wrongly taken by the red bag's record would be enough to disarm it.

## What it means

Version 2 is rejected as it stands, as one wrong merge was agreed to do. What it
shows is that a thing swapped for a look-alike at a place the rover has not
seen empty is still taken as the old thing, and that is ordinary life in a
home. Next, offline: find why the colour veto let the swap through, count wrong
merges across sessions in the scorer, and then make version 3 and freeze it
for another fresh session. Whether M5's sign-off stands, now that criterion 4
is known to fail, is the owner's call.
