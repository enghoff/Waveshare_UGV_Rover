# M3 session 2: the wall back-off works on the rover; the battery ended the run

**The second supervised session ran 11 minutes and 38 m, and the drive's back-off
from a wall worked on the hardware.** A drive refused because the rover stood
inside the costmap's margin recorded "backed off 20 cm to somewhere the planner
will plan from, then arrived". The run did not reach R-AUT-13's ending. It was
still finding things to look at when I stopped it, because the battery had gone
from 70% to about 5% (10.6 V, 3.55 V a cell) in those eleven minutes. Runs carry
no battery floor, by the owner's decision of 2026-10-02, so without the stop it
would have run until the board cut out, away from the charger.
[R-AUT-13](../requirements/autonomy.md#r-aut-13) stays open;
[R-SAFE-16](../requirements/safety.md#r-safe-16) held. The owner was present.

## The run

`run/6e9defc4/1`, opened with 900 s and no action limit, at fbf57b1. It lasted
656 s, 37.9 m and 87 actions over 45 turns. One of 37 geometry looks improved a
placement: `object:380`, by 0.75 m to 0.67 m, the thing session 1 had made worse.
Seven drives failed, never three in a row:

- one START_OCCUPIED refusal, which came part-way along a route after the rover
  had moved, so the back-off deliberately did not run; the next drive backed off
  and arrived;
- three viewpoints where the rover's body does not fit;
- one timeout on a 3.4 m route to a goal 2.5 m away;
- one "no route the rover fits through" after 16 recoveries;
- one Nav2 abort (code 103) after planning a 37.7 m route to a goal 3.9 m away.

I ended the run with `stop_driving` at 5% and drove it 1.1 m back to the charger.

## The gimbal

The aims that took a new picture were 13, 16, 17, 2, 20 and 19 degrees off centre,
because a drive arrives within about 15 degrees of the heading it was asked for.
The 15-degree band of fbf57b1 left the camera still on the two under it and swung
it on the rest. The band is now 25 degrees, and every move's approach undershoot
is 5 degrees rather than 30, measured on the same day
([the measurement](2026-10-05-gimbal-approach-undershoot.md)).

## What it means

The back-off is shown. R-AUT-13's ending needs a session that starts from a full
charge, or a run long enough to run out of things to look at before the battery
does. The drain is the new fact: 65 points in 11 minutes against session 1's 40
in 20. Whether to keep runs unconditioned on the battery is the owner's to revisit;
this run would have ended by cutout, not by going home. The 37.7 m route to a goal
3.9 m away is worth a look in the navigation record before it recurs.
