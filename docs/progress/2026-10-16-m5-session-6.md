# M5 session 6: version 3 kept every record honest on the session and followed all three moves of the wait, but the same run mixed two paintings in session 5, and it does not pass

**Version 3 of [the world model kept per visit](../plans/a-world-model-kept-per-visit.md)
made no wrong merge on session 6, the session it was frozen for, and followed
all three of the session's moves.** That includes the case the session was built
for: the black bean bag was set down 0.3 m from where the red one had stood
until a minute before, and version 3 kept it in its own record. **It does not
pass**, because in the same run one of its records holds two of session 5's
pictures of the snowy-tree painting with the blue painting. The rule agreed with
the owner counts every record
([the decision](../decisions/the-world-model-is-pass-fail-on-honesty-alone.md));
the plan's own wording counted only the session, and by that it passed. This
entry takes the stricter reading; the owner may overrule it.
[R-WS-17](../requirements/world-state.md#r-ws-17) stays proposed.

**Why the merge appeared shows that version 3's clean record was luck.** It
turns its learned likeness into a score with a scale fitted to every pair of
regions in one picture, over all the pictures it is given, later ones included.
Adding 44 pictures that the rover took on its charger at the end of session 5,
after that session had been copied off for development, shifted a score on
2026-10-03 from 0.850 to 0.849. From there the history went another way, and by
session 5 the two painting pictures were "here as before" on the blue
painting's record. Session 6's pictures were not needed for it, and its lidar
recording changed nothing. The same input gives byte-identical filings, so the
cause is not chance at run time.

## The session

`session6-1016`, 14:23-14:39, as planned in
`experiments/m5_changes/staged/session6-planned.json` (`a9e8de8`):

1. The owner set out the four things with the rover switched off on its
   charger, so it never photographed them being carried.
2. The rover was switched on, checked (`session_preflight.py`) and unplugged.
3. Loop A from the charger, living room first, the camera aimed all round from
   every stop. The photo check (`session_check.py`) ran once the rover had left
   the living room and found every thing photographed (the black bean bag in
   two pictures, the minimum to move it).
4. The rover waited in the bedroom two minutes while the owner made the three
   moves.
5. Loop B, a 3.5-minute run, home, 5 cm from the spot.

Every stop's position check stayed within 5 cm. The battery read 90% when
unplugged and 72% at rest afterwards. Nobody was in the living room during
either loop.

Two faults in the session's own scripts, neither of which touched the
session. The pre-flight called the camera dead, because a parked rover
deliberately takes no new pictures of a view it already has and the one boot
picture was stamped before the clock was set; one region-count query proved the
camera and GPU instead. Fetching the world store dropped once over wifi, and the
preparation script read a `D:` path as a remote host.

## What it means

Version 3's rules for moves did what they were built for, on places and a swap
they had not seen. What fails is underneath them: decisions near a threshold
flip when unrelated pictures are added, and a model whose past filing changes
with every new picture cannot be checked once and trusted afterwards. The next
step is to fix the scale with the learned likeness, fitted once on development
pictures, and to measure how much the filing moves when pictures are added or
removed, before any session is spent on it.
