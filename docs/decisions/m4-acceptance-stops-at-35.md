# M4's acceptance stops at 35 pairs, short of the 43 declared

Status: agreed 2026-10-10 by the owner, who stopped the attempts because running
them again later would get nothing new. Changes the count
[M4's criterion 4](../plans/autonomous-curiosity.md#milestone-m4-chosen-viewpoints-measurably-improve-where-things-are-known-to-be)
declared at 43 pairs ([m4-scores-from-the-thing.md](m4-scores-from-the-thing.md));
the things, the score and the analysis stand.

M4's acceptance attempts end at 35 pairs over the eleven taped things, and M4 is
judged on those ([the result](../progress/2026-10-10-m4-acceptance.md)).

## Why

**What was left would mostly repeat what had already been measured.** By the
morning of 2026-10-10 the rover had attempted ten of the eleven taped things.
Most records it had placed as well as it could from anywhere it could stand. A
few it had looked at and found nothing, and these were set aside for two hours,
then tried again with the same result. The floor lamp's records had no viewpoint
it could reach. Of the 21 attempts after the first block, 17 came out as nothing
for both arms. The owner stopped there, and allowed one more run first, from the
living room, where three bedroom records were still open with small predicted
gains (0.1 to 0.2 m). That run made the first attempt on the eleventh thing and
reached one of the three. Its drives to the footboard's and the portrait's
viewpoints were refused with no way past, and afterwards the rover's heading was
30 degrees out, the [arrival-turn fault](../progress/2026-10-09-arrival-heading.md).

**The interval does not hang on the last eight, unless they go badly.** Counted
as nothing for both arms, which is what the set-aside records gave every time,
they would leave the paired difference at +0.27 (95% interval +0.04 to +0.66)
instead of +0.33 (+0.05 to +0.76). If the footboard's and the portrait's
attempts had repeated the worst result each has given (-0.23 and -1.02), it
would be +0.24 (-0.02 to +0.63), just touching zero. The chosen viewpoint's own
gain stays above zero either way, at +0.34 (+0.12 to +0.69) in the worse case.
The stop was decided from what was left to attempt, not from the interval, which
had cleared zero at 19 pairs, when the count was kept at 43.

## What was considered

- **Three more sessions to reach 43,** about two hours apart, each a few minutes
  long and mostly repeats. Rejected by the owner: nothing in the flat would have
  changed between them.
- **Fresh targets:** new things taped, labelled and frozen, so that the remaining
  pairs come from gaps the rover has not closed. Not pursued.

## What would reopen it

- A fault found in how the 35 were scored or labelled that moves the interval
  below zero.
- A later phase that leans on criterion 3's margin. Before it does, the two open
  records are attempted: the footboard's and the portrait's, from the living room.
