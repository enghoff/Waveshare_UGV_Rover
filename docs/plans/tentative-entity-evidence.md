# Test tentative associations without letting them change the model

R-WS-13 remains open; R-WS-17 and R-WS-18 remain proposed. Keep the proposed
one-in-twenty contamination tolerance as a development target, not an accepted
measurement. The failed release/category trials do not require the owner to relax
it before another bounded diagnosis.

## Question and frozen first candidate

Can existing association decisions retain poor views without immediately using
them to refit position or appearance? This experiment changes influence, not the
appearance/geometry gates used to propose a destination. No labels enter decisions.

The ordinary resolver still proposes and records a destination. New observations
joining an existing entity start tentative. They are omitted from position refits
and exemplar updates until their plain and masked DINO similarities both reach
the existing RECOGNISED value (0.70) against two already confirmed observations
from distinct other inference frames. Backend and vector width must agree. Missing
masked evidence abstains. Candidates are rechecked after each resolver pass, so a
later confirmed view can promote a previously tentative one. Promotion is staged:
no observation may use a peer promoted in the same round as its own witness.

The baseline discovery founders remain confirmed, including range singletons.
Inherited before-snapshot evidence also starts confirmed. This deliberately does
not repair bad foundations or inherited contamination. It tests one mechanism,
not a complete entity lifecycle. Tentative rows retain their proposed entity and
claim its frame slot, preventing them from founding duplicates merely because
they have not been promoted. They are available in the archive and the output,
but the prototype does not yet reconsider their proposed destination.

Only confirmed observations can update appearance or position. Promotion applies
the original exemplar updater and triggers the original placement refit. Confirmed
history is filtered before applying its bounded history limit, so a run of tentative
observations cannot push all older confirmed evidence out of that window.

## Reproduction and decision before measuring

First replay the fresh before/after recording with no proxy, using the already
reconstructed recorded schedule, background passes 7, 13, 16, 22, 25, 27 and frozen
map. Require all 4,026 final memberships, placements and exemplar sets to reproduce.
Also replay the longer 516-frame recording unchanged before comparing a candidate.

Use both recordings. Keep all thresholds, schedules and frozen labels unchanged.
Exclude disputed 61656, treat person head/body as one identity, and count each older
pair once. No fitting is needed; report the two older label folds separately, but
do not call them two independent recordings. Report clear and tentative fresh
labels separately. No masked-semantic or phrase category changes enter this trial.

To warrant further integration the candidate must, on each recording:

- reduce wrong confirmed pairs strictly, without introducing a new wrong confirmed
  pair relative to the control;
- retain at least 90% of the control's correct confirmed pairs and at least 95%
  of its correct pairs when tentative associations are included;
- increase neither missing labelled objects nor objects with multiple records
  containing two labelled views, using all proposed associations;
- retain every observation and its measured bytes, and demonstrably exclude every
  tentative observation from both exemplar updates and position refits.

Report confirmed coverage, tentative coverage, pending views, per-object changes,
new wrong hypothetical relations and intermediate checkpoints. A pair improvement
does not establish the 5% observation-level target. Neither recording constitutes
independent acceptance. Failure rejects this candidate, not the distinction between
tentative and confirmed evidence. A favourable result still needs blind evaluation,
reconsideration of tentative destinations, bad-founder diagnosis and hardware proof.

Only workstation experiments and documentation will change in this trial. Production
integration and deployment follow only if its evidence warrants them.
